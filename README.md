# Case 1 – Amoxicillin

## Adverse Reaction

Skin rash and itching.

## Seriousness

**Non-serious**

### Reason

The reported reaction is skin rash and itching. There is no information indicating hospitalization, life-threatening condition, disability or death.

Therefore, the case is classified as **Non-serious**.

## Expectedness

**Expected**

Skin rash and itching are recognized adverse reactions associated with amoxicillin.

### Final Classification

**Amoxicillin → Skin rash & itching → Non-serious → Expected**

# Severity vs Seriousness

## Severity

Severity describes the intensity of an adverse reaction.

Examples:

- Mild
- Moderate
- Severe

## Seriousness

Seriousness is based on specific clinical or regulatory outcomes, such as:

- Death
- Life-threatening condition
- Hospitalization
- Prolongation of hospitalization
- Significant disability
- Congenital anomaly
- Other medically important condition

Severity and seriousness are therefore not the same.
